# Employee Handbook

Welcome to our company! This handbook provides essential information about our policies, procedures, and expectations for all employees.

## Working Hours

Our standard working hours are designed to promote productivity while maintaining work-life balance.

### Regular Schedule

The standard work week is Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours when all employees should be available are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

### Flexible Working

Employees may request flexible working arrangements after completing their probationary period. Flexible options include adjusted start/end times and remote work days, subject to manager approval.

### Overtime Policy

Overtime work must be pre-approved by your direct supervisor. Non-exempt employees will receive overtime compensation at 1.5 times their regular hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 per week.

## Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is essential for team collaboration and business operations.

### Reporting Absences

If you are unable to attend work, notify your supervisor at least one hour before your scheduled start time. For planned absences, submit a leave request through the HR system at least 3 business days in advance.

### Tardiness

Employees are expected to arrive on time. Repeated tardiness may result in disciplinary action. Three instances of tardiness within a 30-day period will trigger a formal review.

### Remote Work Guidelines

When working remotely, employees must be available during core hours, maintain a professional workspace, and ensure reliable internet connectivity. All remote work must be logged in the attendance system.

## Leave Benefits

We offer comprehensive leave benefits to support employee well-being.

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued monthly. Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days to the following year.

### Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

### Parental Leave

New parents are eligible for parental leave: 12 weeks for primary caregivers and 4 weeks for secondary caregivers. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain professional standards and ethical behavior.

### Professional Behavior

Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

### Confidentiality

Employees must protect company confidential information and trade secrets. Do not share sensitive business information with unauthorized parties, including on social media.

### Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager and HR. Outside employment or business activities that compete with company interests are prohibited.

## Performance Management

We are committed to helping employees grow and succeed in their roles.

### Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews are conducted annually, with informal check-ins quarterly. Reviews assess goal achievement, competencies, and professional development.

### Goal Setting

At the beginning of each year, employees work with their managers to set SMART goals aligned with team and company objectives.

### Professional Development

The company provides an annual learning budget of $1,500 per employee for courses, certifications, and conferences relevant to their role.
